Culinary Guidelines

Heating & Fridge Storage

♨️ Microwave Instructions

1. Remove the clear lid or lift one corner to vent steam.
2. Microwave on HIGH for 2 to 3 minutes (depending on microwave wattage).
3. Let rest for 1 minute before removing. Fluff rice or potatoes with a fork and enjoy!

🔥 Oven Reheating

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Transfer meal contents to an oven-safe baking dish or tray.
3. Cover loosely with aluminum foil and heat for 10 to 12 minutes until piping hot.

Fridge Shelf Life: Meals stay perfectly fresh in your refrigerator for up to 5 to 6 days from the delivery batch day. You can also freeze meals upon arrival for up to 30 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a 6-meal minimum?

Batch cooking allows us to source premium Ontario meats, genuine Greek Dodoni feta, and cold-pressed Kalamata olive oil at optimal freshness and volume price points. The 6-meal minimum ensures batch integrity and gives you a structured week of meals starting at just $59.94 CAD.

How does the 10% volume discount work?

When your cart contains 7 or more meals, our cart engine automatically deducts 10% from the food subtotal. There is no coupon code needed.

What if I am not home during delivery?

Our courier leaves your order at your front door, concierge, or designated drop spot in insulated thermal cooler bags packed with frozen gel packs. Food stays safe and chilled for up to 6 hours after delivery.
