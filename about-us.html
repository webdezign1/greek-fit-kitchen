About Greek Fit Kitchen

Why sacrifice flavor to stay in peak shape? Meet George the Greek and discover how our Etobicoke kitchen is redefining athletic meal prep in Ontario.

"Greek Food is Nature's Original Fitness Diet"

Growing up in a traditional Greek family, every single meal was built around three non-negotiables: fresh lean proteins, cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil from ancient olive groves, and wild mountain herbs hand-picked under the Mediterranean sun.

When founder George the Greek became dedicated to fitness and athletic training in Toronto, he experienced the same frustration so many athletes and busy professionals face: conventional fitness meal prep was dry, bland, repetitive, and cooked with cheap seed oils that caused systemic inflammation.

In partnership with our state-of-the-art Etobicoke commercial kitchen facility (3575 Lake Shore Blvd W), Greek Fit Kitchen was created to bring culinary excitement back to performance eating.

Our Four Kitchen Commitments

  • 1
    Never Frozen: We batch-cook twice a week (Sundays & Wednesdays). Your meals are never frozen in a commercial freezer or thawed before delivery.
  • 2
    Local Ontario Poultry & AAA Beef: We partner with local butchers for lean breast tenderloins, fresh Atlantic salmon fillets, and Canadian AAA sirloin cuts.
  • 3
    Custom Starches & Veggies: Every dish comes with 2 sides chosen by you. We never lock you into fixed carb or vegetable ratios.
  • 4
    Eco-Friendly Recyclable Packaging: Packed in BPA-free, leak-resistant, microwave-safe and oven-safe recyclable trays.
